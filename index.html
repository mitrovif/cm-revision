Who census questions identify

How the census questions in the draft identify refugees, IDPs and stateless persons under the IRRS, IRIS and IROSS recommendations, where inclusion and exclusion errors occur, and how the draft's points compare with suggested changes.

Based on the UN Principles and Recommendations for Population and Housing Censuses, Revision 4, and the International Recommendations on Refugee Statistics (IRRS), IDP Statistics (IRIS) and Statelessness Statistics (IROSS). Errors are marked as already in the Compilers' Manual draft or as suggested additions.
